# 1. Research

## Goal

Understand the real problem, the users, the risks, and what “good” looks like before writing requirements.

## Activities

- Interview stakeholders and operators
- Map current (manual or scripted) process
- Identify data sources, legacy systems, and quality issues
- List regulatory, safety, or financial constraints
- Draft success metrics and failure costs

## Key artifact

**Research brief** (1–3 pages):

- Problem statement
- Who is affected and who owns the outcome
- Success metrics (and how they will be measured)
- Known risks and constraints
- Open questions

## Exit gate

Stakeholders agree on the problem and the success metrics.
