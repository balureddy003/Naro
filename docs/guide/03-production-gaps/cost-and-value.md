# Unclear Business Value & Cost

## The gap

Token and infrastructure cost grow without a clear link to business outcomes. No measurement of whether the agent is worth running.

## What “closed” looks like

- Token and cost budgets enforced at runtime.
- Evaluation metrics tied to business outcomes.
- Cost attribution per run or per agent.

## How Naro addresses it

- Token and cost budgets exist on the runner.
- Benchmarks and evidence records give outcome data that can be joined to cost.

## What remains application-owned

- Defining the business metrics.
- Wiring cost export into your finance or observability stack.
- Setting and reviewing the actual budget numbers.

## Checklist

- [ ] Cost / token budget is configured.
- [ ] At least one business outcome is measured per run.
- [ ] Cost is reviewed against value on a regular cadence.
